Privacy Policy — Charlie Calls

Charlie Calls is a voice app where you talk in English with Charlie, a fictional character. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect. Your email when you sign up (via Google or a sign-in link). The text of your conversations with Charlie, so that he can remember you between calls. Usage counts (how many messages you sent) to apply free limits. If you subscribe, payment is handled by Lemon Squeezy; we do not see your card details.

Your voice. While you talk, audio is streamed to Deepgram for speech-to-text and to our server only as text. We do not store audio recordings. Charlie's voice is generated by Microsoft Azure Speech. Text you say is sent to an AI model (Alibaba Cloud Qwen) to generate Charlie's reply.

Guests. If you try Charlie without an account, we store a browser fingerprint hash to count your free replies. Nothing else.

Deleting your data. Email skaylet2007@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, and we delete your account, conversations and memories within 7 days.
